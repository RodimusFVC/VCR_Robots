TRIAGE OF THE 6 MISMATCHES
  spf_q / samp_per_frame fallbacks : INERT. Only used when header_valid is false. Header is valid.
  AUD_CREDIT_MAX                   : audio only. Credit pegs at the cap either way in steady play.
  FRAME_DIV                        : benign/better. 75 Hz gives MORE fetch opportunities than 30 Hz.
  WD_LIMIT                         : DO NOT FIX. Wrong on paper, but a true 50 ms produced green
                                     snow across all playback (HW, 2026-08-18). The 8 ms abort is
                                     load-bearing -- evidence the memory path is stressed.
  LINE TIME                        : the only video-relevant, untested one.  <-- CHANGE APPLIED

CHANGE APPLIED 2026-08-18 (VCR-Robots.sv, fb_raster_reader instantiation)
  .H_BP  24 -> 168   (added; was the module default)   H_TOTAL 576 -> 720
  .V_BP 212 -> 67                                      V_TOTAL 724 -> 579
  line time  46.08 us -> 57.60 us   (exactly the 40 MHz proven value)
  refresh    29.9846 Hz vs film 29.97003, ratio 1.00049 (same trade the 40 MHz build accepted)
  cycles per DDR request  36 -> 45  (40 MHz known-good was 18)
  REVERT: delete the .H_BP line, set .V_BP back to 16'd212

WHY THIS ONE: the screenshot's garbage band across the bottom of the frame is the
"fill-stall signature" documented in VCR-Robots.sv itself -- the reader must fetch
H_ACT/4 = 128 words inside ONE line-time; if it misses, fline falls behind vcnt and
the bottom of the framebuffer is never scanned out. The 100 MHz comment claimed the
change gave "MORE cycles per line (4608 vs 2304)" -- true in cycles, but line time is
H_TOTAL/pixel_clock, and the pixel clock rose 10.0 -> 12.5 MHz, so wall-clock time per
line FELL 20% below the configuration that was proven good.
